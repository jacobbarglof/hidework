# Feature gaps (2026-11-07)

Goal from the founder: Hidework should have every feature a leatherworker or pattern maker could need. This list compares the current code (read on 2026-11-07) against what leatherworkers do in practice and what CLO3D, Marvelous Designer, Seamly2D/Valentina, Lectra/Gerber, Inkscape/Illustrator/Fusion, LightBurn, xTool, Glowforge, Cricut and Deepnest offer. It extends `docs/research-2026-11-feature-survey.md`.

**Counted as being built, so not listed:** XPBD solver with floor contact and self-collision, the later wgpu backend, Fusion-style ribbon, typed command line, full shortcut set, 3D-only and pattern-only views, copy/cut/paste, shift-click multi-select, merge pieces, align/distribute, grid snap, the 35 to 40 template library, and print layout packing onto the fewest pages.

**Key:** In Hidework = yes / partial / no, with the file that shows it. P0 = must-have for a public release to the leather community, P1 = strong, P2 = nice. Size: S under 2 days, M under a week, L more than a week (one agent).

## Pattern drafting

| Feature | Why a leatherworker needs it | In Hidework | P | Size | Implementation note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internal lines (placement, stitch, crease, cut, fold kinds) | Pocket placement lines, decorative stitching and patch outlines sit inside a piece, not on its edge. | no: `model.rs` Piece has only edges, folds, holes, marks | P0 | M | `Piece.internal_lines: Vec<InternalLine{edges, kind}>` in model.rs; compute.rs makes stitch runs for `Stitch` kind; sheet.rs and fab.rs draw them; canvas tool. |
| Partial stitch runs (start/stop along an edge, stitch an internal line) | Pocket tops, open ends and stitch-down tabs stop short of the corner. | partial: `Edge.stitched` is whole-edge only (`model.rs`) | P0 | S | Add `stitch_from_mm/stitch_to_mm` on Edge; compute.rs `StitchRun` already handles open runs. |

| Offset path tool (new piece or internal line at distance) | Stitch guides, stiffeners and inset liners are offsets of the outline. | partial: lining inset only (`generators.rs` lining) | P1 | S | Expose `geom::offset_closed` as a tool producing a piece or internal line; ops in drafting.ts. |

| Free rotate and scale with numeric entry and pivot | Templates from the web arrive at odd angles; resizing a design to a new card or phone size. | partial: rotate 90 degrees, flip (`ui/src/ops.ts` rotatePiece) | P0 | S | `transformPiece(angle, scale, pivot)` in ops.ts; rotate holes, marks, hardware, grain; MCP `transform_piece` gains angle/scale. |

| Size runs and grading | Belts, watch straps, dog collars and bags in S/M/L from one pattern. | no | P2 | L | Builds on parametric dimensions: `Project.sizes` table; export one sheet per size. |
| Pleats and accordion gussets | Bellows pockets and expanding gussets. | partial: darts only (`generators.rs` add_dart) | P2 | M | Pleat as a set of fold lines with fold direction; compute.rs reuses fold compensation. |

| Persistent dimensions printed on the template | Makers check finished sizes against the print with a ruler. | partial: transient measure tool (`ui/src/canvas.ts` measure) | P1 | S | `Project.dimensions` (two anchors + offset); sheet.rs draws them. |

## Leathercraft specifics

| Feature | Why a leatherworker needs it | In Hidework | P | Size | Implementation note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pricking iron catalogue by brand and style | Makers own specific irons: Vergez Blanchard #7 3.85, #8 3.38, #10 2.70, #12 2.25 mm; Amy Roke #9 3.0 mm; French, diamond, round; tooth counts. | partial: free pitch number (`model.rs` StitchSettings.pitch_mm) | P0 | S | `craft::irons()` with brand, size number, pitch, style, tooth counts, SPI; stitch panel picks an iron. |

| Design rule check | Catches mistakes before cutting leather: rivet too near an edge, hole on the stitch line, hardware over a seam, unsewn edges, slot narrower than its strap, firm veg-tan folded too thick without a skive. | partial: join length and hole-count checks (`compute.rs` JoinReport) | P0 | M | New `drc.rs` returning structured issues with piece/edge refs; issues list panel; MCP `check_design`. |
| Contents fit check | Pocket inner width vs card 86 × 54 mm or phone size plus ease and leather thickness. | partial: fold compensation for contents (`model.rs` Fold.contents_mm) | P1 | S | Part of drc.rs: compare pocket stitch-line interior to declared contents. |
| Stack thickness and fastener length | Choosing rivet, Chicago screw and snap post lengths needs the total layers at that point, including folds and laminates. | no | P1 | M | New `stack.rs`: layers at a point from pieces, laminates, folds; craft.rs hardware gets post-length ranges; BOM lists the right size. |

| Hole-by-hole matching across seams | Shows which hole meets which, fixes start offsets and handles curved-to-straight seams. | partial: counts and lengths compared (`compute.rs` 587-605) | P1 | M | Pair holes by arc length in compute.rs; draw matching numbers every 10 holes; "copy holes from the other edge". |
| Skiving map with depth and profile | Makers skive to a remaining thickness (edge bevel, flat skive, fold skive, whole-piece split). | partial: skive band width per edge (`model.rs` Edge.skive_mm) | P1 | M | `Skive{width, remaining_mm, profile}` on edges and folds; sheet.rs legend; stack.rs and sim use reduced thickness. |
| Area units dm², m², ft² and sale units (side, shoulder, belly, panel) | European tanneries sell by dm² or m²; US by ft²; small makers buy panels. | partial: ft² only (`bom.rs` MM2_PER_SQFT) | P1 | S | Units preference in bom.rs/costing; material gains `sold_as` and size. |
| Hide model: outline, flaws, quality zones, backbone line | Real hides have scars, brands, tick bites, loose belly; straps must run along the backbone. | no | P1 | L | New `hide.rs`: outline (drawn, imported or traced from a photo), flaw polygons, zones A/B/C, spine vector, measured area vs stamped area. |
| Nesting on a hide or sheet with grain and zone rules | Yield on an irregular hide is the main material cost; Lectra claims up to 7% better yield from nesting. | no | P1 | L | New `nest.rs`: no-fit-polygon nesting (port the SVGnest/Deepnest method, MIT), rotation limited by grain tolerance, pieces restricted to zones, avoid flaws; report yield %. |
| Cost and price calculator | Makers price by materials + hardware + labour hours × rate + overhead, then wholesale and retail markup. | partial: leather cost from price per ft² (`bom.rs` total_cost) | P1 | S | New `costing.rs`: hardware and thread prices per item, labour estimate (per hole, per edge length), margin; cost panel. |
| User-defined hardware | Makers buy hardware not in a fixed catalogue of 23. | partial: fixed catalogue (`craft.rs` hardware) | P1 | S | `Project.custom_hardware` with hole layout, weight, price; craft.rs merges it. |
| Moulding allowance for wet-moulded pieces | A moulded sheath or tray needs extra pattern size for the depth it is pressed into. | no (glasses-case template only mentions moulding, `library.rs`) | P1 | M | Mould depth per piece; compute.rs grows the cut line by developed depth; pairs with the mould STL below. |
| Tooling artwork transfer | Carvers trace a design onto cased leather from a 1:1 print on tracing film. | partial: preset tooling patterns by name (`model.rs` MarkKind::Tooling) | P1 | M | Mark can hold imported SVG artwork; print artwork alone at 1:1, mirrored option; laser score of carving lines. |
| Edge bevel and edge finish recipe | Bevel size depends on thickness; finish steps feed the instructions. | partial: finish kinds (`model.rs` EdgeFinish, `craft.rs` finishes) | P2 | S | Bevel number per thickness table in craft.rs; recipe text into instruction steps. |
| Named tannery leather presets | Makers think in "Buttero 4 oz", "Chromexcel 5 oz", not generic veg-tan. | partial: generic presets (`physics.rs` presets) | P2 | S | Add presets with typical thickness ranges; physics stays marked as estimates. |
| Machine stitch spec (SPI, needle, thread tex/ticket) | Machine stitchers set stitch length and thread size, not iron pitch. | partial: Lockstitch kind (`model.rs` StitchKind) | P2 | S | Fields on StitchSettings shown only for Lockstitch; thread catalogue gains tex sizes. |

## Suggested work packages

**Step 0 (one agent, half a day, before the others): schema commit.** Add every new optional field from the tables above to `model.rs` and mirror it in `ui/src/types.ts`, all `#[serde(default)]`, no behaviour: `Edge.notches/allowance_mm/stitch_from_mm/stitch_to_mm/skive`, `Fold.line`, `Piece.internal_lines/cutouts/symmetry/derived/hidden/locked`, `StitchSettings.spacing/iron`, `Project.underlays/hides/colorways/dimensions/custom_hardware/steps/licence`.
